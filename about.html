The GarrettPrep Philosophy

SAT improvement is not magic. It is training. The same way an athlete improves by repeating the right movement under pressure, a student improves by repeating the right test-taking patterns under timing pressure.

1) Diagnose✕ Stop guessing what the problem is. Find the exact section, question type, and habit costing points.
→
2) Teach The Move✓ Replace confusion with a repeatable strategy, rule, shortcut, or elimination pattern.
→
3) Drill Under Time✓ Practice until the student can execute when the clock is running.

What Students Get

  • ✓Full SAT support: Reading, Writing, Math, and strategy.
  • ✓Whiteboard-style explanations that simplify hard questions.
  • ✓Timed drills that build speed and confidence.
  • ✓Error tracking so missed questions become a clear action plan.
  • ✓Accountability for homework, attendance, and practice-test follow-through.

What GarrettPrep Is Not

  • ✕Not random worksheets with no plan.
  • ✕Not passive lectures where the student just watches.
  • ✕Not vague advice like “just practice more.”
  • ✕Not one-size-fits-all prep for every student.
  • ✕Not for students who refuse to do the reps.
