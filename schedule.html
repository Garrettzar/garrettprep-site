What The Summer Schedule Covers

This is full SAT preparation, not a math-only program.

Reading

Passage strategy, evidence questions, main idea, inference, vocabulary-in-context, and trap answer elimination.

Writing

Grammar rules, punctuation, transitions, sentence structure, rhetorical skills, and fast elimination patterns.

Math

Algebra, functions, geometry, data analysis, percent traps, and shortcut-based problem solving.

Strategy

Timing, module pacing, educated guessing, review routines, test-day execution, and confidence under pressure.

Sample Weekly Rhythm

  • ✓Content Session: teach one high-value SAT skill or question type.
  • ✓Timed Drill: practice under pressure with section-specific timing.
  • ✓Error Review: track exactly why points were lost.
  • ✓Homework: targeted Reading, Writing, or Math practice between sessions.

This is a flexible summer structure. Replace with exact meeting times when you finalize availability.
